# Software Engineer II

**Department:** Information Technology
**Institution:** The University of Texas System Administration
**Location:** Austin, TX (hybrid, 3 days on site)
**FLSA Status:** Exempt
**Salary Range:** $105,000 – $130,000

## Purpose of Position

Design, build and maintain the backend services and APIs behind the institution's
internal business applications. Works within an established engineering team,
owns features end to end, and participates in code review, on-call rotation and
release management.

## Essential Functions

- Build and maintain RESTful web services consumed by internal web and mobile
  applications.
- Design and evolve relational database schemas, and write and tune the queries
  that run against them.
- Write automated unit and integration tests, and keep the suite green.
- Review peers' pull requests and respond to review feedback on your own.
- Participate in deployment and release activities, including a shared on-call
  rotation.

## Required Qualifications

Each item is a minimum. A candidate must satisfy every one to be considered.

1. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer
   Engineering, or a closely related technical field.
2. Three (3) or more years of professional, full-time software development
   experience after graduation.
3. Two (2) or more years building and maintaining production REST APIs.
4. Professional proficiency in at least one of: Python, Java, or C#.
5. Two (2) or more years working with a relational database — PostgreSQL,
   MySQL, SQL Server, or Oracle — including schema design and query tuning.
6. Demonstrated use of Git for version control, including branching and pull
   request review.
7. Demonstrated experience writing automated tests, both unit and integration.

## Preferred Qualifications

These do not gate consideration. They distinguish candidates who already meet
every required qualification above.

1. Five (5) or more years of professional software development experience.
2. Production experience with Docker containers and Kubernetes orchestration.
3. Two (2) or more years on a major public cloud platform — Microsoft Azure,
   Amazon Web Services, or Google Cloud Platform.
4. Experience configuring and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps,
   GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins).
5. Production experience with event-driven or message-queue architecture —
   Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, or Azure Service Bus.
6. Experience formally mentoring junior engineers or leading a project team of
   three (3) or more.
7. Master's degree in Computer Science or a closely related technical field.

## Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

- Clear written and verbal communication with both technical and non-technical
  colleagues.
- Ability to estimate work, and to flag risk and slippage early.
- Willingness to work in an established codebase and follow existing convention.

## Working Conditions

Work is performed in an office environment. Uses standard office equipment.
Occasional after-hours work during release windows and on-call rotation.
